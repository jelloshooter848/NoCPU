Acceptance: visible P1/P2 vertical tracks stay outside the original raster,
below the court in portrait and beside it in landscape. Targets are at least
64 px wide, with at least 8 px court separation; travel is preferably 144 px,
with a compact 120 px fallback. The thumb and visible endpoints match the
full normalized paddle range. Both players can drag independently; canceled
pointers, menus, blur, reset and resize cannot leave held inputs. Court touches
never move either paddle. Keep title/game/menu states, compact coin/menu, safe
areas and no outer document scrolling. Layout review covers 320 × 568,
390 × 664, 390 × 844, 844 × 390, 568 × 320, 1280 × 720 and 1920 × 1080.

The verified public 0.2.1 preview is bound to source
`b13495c6146f6b2e0a1bc2f82f173a5ecb3ad175`; the earlier 0.2.0 evidence remains
historical rather than being relabeled. The reported iPhone
obstruction is limited usability feedback, not complete physical-phone
qualification. Final tag readiness still requires actual Android Chrome and
iPhone Safari play, speaker sound, lifecycle and sustained performance for
the complete new runtime. See [validation](0.2.1_VALIDATION.md).
