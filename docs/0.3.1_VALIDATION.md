## Delivery contract

The user authorized proceeding to physical iPhone/Android/controller
qualification before more game ports. A minimal opt-in `?debug` Start/Finish/
Export flow prepares measurable reports on the phone itself. It preserves
normal play, the Pong circuit, integer simulation clock and circuit PCM;
Pong remains the only playable browser game. It adds no game or ROM payload.

The collector must distinguish whole-session elapsed time from native-running
foreground active time, preserve raw start/end measurements and expose missing
or unreliable measurement/identity coverage. Exported metrics do not identify
physical hardware or prove speaker playback. The phone report separately
records actual model, OS/browser, touch/sound/lifecycle/app observations and
the exact tested source/runtime. A real controller report covers only the
tested pairing and stays separate from the existing phone release gate.

The quantitative phone gates remain at least 600 seconds of active play and
simulation/wall ratio ≥0.95. Frame pacing, underruns, memory and input response
remain required evidence with their measurement scope stated; this tool does
not create additional cutoffs. Missing observations remain pending or
unavailable. Compatible-ROM file-picker/retention checks apply only where the
tester has their own legitimately available files.
