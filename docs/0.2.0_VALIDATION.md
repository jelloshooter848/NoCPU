## Physical-phone evidence

For each platform, record the phone model, operating system and browser version,
candidate build identifier and the deployed manifest's full runtime SHA-256,
test date, exact steps and observed results. Play
for at least ten minutes and check coin-start, both touch paddles, rallies,
scoring, audible circuit sound, pause/resume, reset, orientation changes and
returning after backgrounding. Record sustained simulation speed, frame pacing,
audio interruptions, input response and starting/ending memory measurements.
The [release-readiness record](../tests/release-readiness.json) requires at least
600 seconds of active play and a simulation/wall-time ratio of at least 0.95 on
both actual platforms. Record thermal or memory symptoms and agree a supported
device floor from these measurements.

Automated Android/iPhone viewport or browser-engine runs cover useful frontend
behavior. They do not establish physical touch ergonomics, mobile Safari audio,
speaker playback, thermal behavior or phone throughput. Pending physical-device
gates remain pending even when all automated checks pass.

A preview deployment may supply the playable URL needed for these tests. Its
manifest must identify its exact source revision and preview status. Publishing
the final version requires the separately recorded release-readiness gates.

An earlier integrated report failed because portable native produced no video
in its second epoch. Review traced this to the original supply-chip
constructor's uninitialized `custom_update` pointer: reused allocator memory
could incorrectly identify a supply chip as the deoptimizer and suppress its
connections. The prepared copy initializes that pointer to null; the archival
source remains unchanged. Independent optimized four-epoch and allocator
perturbation checks pass after the repair. The independent reviewer also
approved the fresh canonical native/Wasm comparison and ABI challenges, with no
remaining finding in that scope. Its repair record is
`build/audit-reset2/reviewed-fix.json`.

ABI boundary challenges pass for creation, bounded and zero-duration advances,
destruction, clamped inputs and reset with held coin/extreme paddle axes.
Replay logs are under `build/final-validation-core/`. Generated samples and
headless frame comparisons do not establish physical phone speaker playback.

Initial browser testing also exposed keyboard focus handling and audio queue
flushing on repeated unlock. The first complete 50-case browser matrix returned
41 passes, three honest simultaneous-touch skips outside Chromium, and six
failures. Test setup repairs now wait for sound readiness and use an actual
missing-Wasm HTTP response instead of unsupported Firefox worker interception.
Two actual frontend issues were routed for repair/re-review: Firefox audio resume
can outlast the activation cutoff, and slow WebKit presentation callbacks lose
simulation time with a fixed per-callback step cap. Eight-second samples measured
simulation/wall ratios of 0.236 for WebKit and 0.404 for the emulated iPhone,
despite fast core execution; these are failing pacing results, not qualified
phone performance. Chromium, Firefox and emulated Android samples measured
0.992, 0.967 and 0.998 respectively. The complete original attempt and metrics
are retained under `build/browser-before-test-repairs/`; the repaired matrix
must pass before preview deployment.

The independent pacing reviewer reproduced the old scheduler losing simulation
time at four presentation callbacks per second: four wall-clock seconds
advanced only 0.267 simulated seconds, despite 54 ms of worker execution. The
repair follows elapsed active time while keeping one bounded advance in flight
and discarding hidden/paused time. Under the same forced four-callback rate,
Chromium, WebKit and the emulated iPhone measured simulation/wall ratios of
1.00127, 1.00125 and 1.00349. Targeted pause/resume, hidden-page, held-key,
repeated-reset, reset/resume race and 600 ms main-thread-stall challenges pass,
with bounded PCM discard and zero core overruns. These four-second automated
challenges are recorded in `build/audit-timing/repaired-report.json`; broader
matrix results follow below. Reset at zero simulation time is a fresh
unsettled circuit, not a claim of already settled attract mode while paused.
