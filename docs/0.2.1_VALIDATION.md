## Evidence procedure

The candidate must preserve the original 4:3 Pong raster, circuit and sound,
while moving touch input to independent external tracks. Verify visible
endpoint/full-range mapping and thumb feedback, simultaneous player ownership,
court touches having no paddle effect, cancel/menu/blur/reset/resize release,
safe-area fit, orientation and reachable game panels. Record actual tested
source, complete runtime digest, toolchain provenance and browser results.
Emulated Android/iPhone viewports remain separate from physical devices.

Current component-license evidence retains its explicitly named 0.2.0 baseline:
unchanged original-derived Pong, linked SDK families, dependencies and notice
bytes. New artifacts must supply fresh core/web provenance, complete applicable
notices and matching `source/nocpu-0.2.1-source.tar.gz`; retained historical
evidence hashes do not claim a fresh 0.2.1 build.

## Physical-phone qualification

Use the actual published 0.2.1 preview after deployment. Record model, OS,
Android Chrome or iPhone Safari version, date, build ID and complete runtime
digest. Repeat coin/start, two simultaneous paddle tracks, rally/scoring,
audible circuit sound, pause/resume/reset, mute/unmute/recovery, portrait and
landscape, pointer cancellation and background/recovery. Confirm fingers stay
off the raster and both external tracks remain reachable inside safe areas.

Record at least 600 seconds of sustained play and a simulation/wall ratio of
at least 0.95, with frame pacing, audio underruns, memory start/end and input
response. The existing [readiness record](../tests/release-readiness.json)
remains pending until actual results satisfy every required check. Apply the
detailed [phone procedure](0.2.0_VALIDATION.md#physical-phone-evidence) to these
new runtime bytes, not the earlier preview. A final tag requires reviewed,
complete runtime-bound evidence; preparing or publishing a preview does not
satisfy it.
