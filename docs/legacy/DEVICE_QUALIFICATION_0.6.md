Physical results remain pending. Use this packet with the
[report template](../DEVICE_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md), [retained Pong procedure](../DEVICE_QUALIFICATION.md)
and [Home Screen procedure](DEVICE_QUALIFICATION_0.5.md). Identify the actual

## Existing games and installed iPhone

Retain the Pong ≥600 active-second, ≥0.95 simulation/wall baseline and all eight
manual checks. Separately test all four Pong Doubles paddles and its one-/two-coin
starts, Space Race's two digital controls and Coin/Start, and Breakout's shared
paddle, held Serve, distinct credit-dependent starts and options. A Pong pass
does not qualify another title or runtime. Test speaker audio only for circuits
with implemented audio.

For identified iPhone Safari and installed contexts separately, record
portrait/landscape safe areas, held-input rotation/cancellation, menu/file-picker
access, background/lock/audio interruption recovery, denied/evicted storage and
cold relaunch. Fullscreen/orientation lock are not prerequisites. Preferences
may restore; circuit save-state and shared/permanent storage are not promised.
Online Home Screen installation is distinct from offline availability.

## Stunt Cycle and Indy 4

Supply compatible private ROMs locally; record accepted descriptor names,
sizes and checksums without attaching payloads to public reports. Test missing,
incompatible and removed ROM recovery, re-import/export and optional retention
in browser and installed contexts. Confirm all required files before Play.

Record Stunt Cycle throttle and original Coin/Start operation, representative
jumps/misses/game ending, options readback and reset preservation. Record each
free-game threshold actually exercised; available menu options alone do not
prove their awards. Record Indy 4's four independent steering/accelerator slots,
all five Green/Cyan/Violet/Red/Dollar coin channels, four held Hand Brake
inputs, representative race/contact/timer behavior and timer options. Indy 4
has no Start switch; preserve its original coin-driven start behavior.
Use suitable actual controllers and layouts for multiplayer; do not assume
four people can share a phone comfortably. Record controller identity, assigned
slots, neutral/reconnect and menu/reset/background input release.

Both games have no active mono-output hookup in original DICE 0.9. Confirm that
the interface discloses inherited unavailable audio and that no replacement
sound is fabricated. Silence cannot qualify physical speaker fidelity. Keep
long-session/performance and any missing hardware-model evidence explicit.

New-title evidence is title/options specific; no additional mandatory
600-second threshold for every title is implied. Physical readiness and final
tags remain pending until the required actual evidence and authorization exist.
